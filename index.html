Sherbet

Points for your kids,
and good days to spend them on.

Points arrive every morning for every child, the same number for each of them. You add more for the things you notice.

It remembers what you promised

When points buy a trip to the cinema, Sherbet knows whether you have actually been — and it keeps asking until you have. It is the only family app that holds the parent to account rather than the child.

Two taps, and the reason is kept

A kind word to a brother. Homework without three reminders. Owning up to something hard. You notice it, you tap it, and the reason is written down — so a year from now there is a record of every time somebody noticed them.

Your family's data stays yours

No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Everything lives in your own iCloud. We never see it.
