Privacy

The short version: we do not have your family's data. It is not on a server of ours, because we do not have one.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Sherbet has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers. We cannot see your children's names, their points, the reasons you write, or anything else you put into the app — not because we promise not to look, but because it never reaches us.

Where your data actually lives

On your devices, and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. That is what lets a point given on your phone appear on your child's iPad. It is your iCloud storage, under your Apple ID, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

If you turn iCloud off, the app keeps working and everything stays on that one device.

Children's information

Sherbet is designed for a parent to use, and for children to see their own points. A parent enters a child's first name and chooses a cartoon face. That is all the app asks for. There is no photograph, no date of birth, no email address, no location, and no way for a child to communicate with anyone outside the family.

All of it stays in the family's own iCloud. We do not receive it, store it, analyse it or share it, and there is nothing for us to sell.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, your billing address, or your name on the card. We are told only whether a subscription is active.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics service, no crash reporting service, no advertising network, no marketing tools. The only other party involved is Apple, because the app runs on their platform and uses their iCloud.

Deleting everything

Delete the app from each device, then remove Sherbet's data from your iCloud under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing to request from us and nobody to ask.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 you have rights to access and correct personal information held about you. In our case the honest answer is that we hold none. Your data is in your own iCloud and you already have full access to it.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if a future version needs a server for a feature — we will say so here plainly, and in the app, before it happens rather than afterwards.
