Common questions

Points are not appearing on my child's iPad

Sherbet syncs through iCloud, so every device has to be signed into the same Apple ID. On each device check Settings → tap the name at the very top. If a child's iPad uses their own Apple ID, it will not sync yet — family sharing across separate accounts is coming.

Otherwise: make sure both devices are online, and tap the cloud icon at the top of the parent screen to force a sync.

Why can't I take away all of a child's points?

Because some of them were never earned. Every child gets points each morning simply for being here, and those are protected — no adult, and no setting, can remove them. Only points an adult gave can be taken back. That is deliberate, and it is the part of the app we would least like to change.

Can grandparents give points?

That is what the paid tier is for: more adults as well as more children.

My child changed their character and I preferred the old one

It is theirs. Ask them nicely.

Can I get my data out?

It is already yours — it lives in your iCloud, not ours. A proper export is on the list.

Something is broken

Tell us what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. That is genuinely enough, and it is more useful than a screenshot on its own.
