Common questions

Points are not appearing on my child's iPad

Sherbet syncs through iCloud, so devices on the same Apple ID find each other on their own. On each device check Settings → tap the name at the very top.

A device on a different Apple ID — the other parent's phone, or a child's iPad on their own account — joins by invitation instead. On a device that is already set up: tap the gear at the top right, then the row with your own name on it, then Invite a grown-up. That makes a place with their name on it and a six-character code that opens it, good once. They get the same family, the same children and the same history, from their own account.

If everything is on one account and a device still looks behind: make sure both are online, then tap the gear at the top right, tap the iCloud row, and press Upload everything again. Nothing is duplicated and nothing is lost.

Why can't I take away all of a child's points?

Some of them arrive on their own. Every child gets the same number each morning, and those ones stay — no adult and no setting removes them. Only points an adult gave can be taken back.

Can grandparents give points?

Yes, and it is the best thing the app does. Free Sherbet is one grown-up, on every device they own; Sherbet Family is one payment, once, and takes that to ten — a second parent, grandparents, anybody who helps run the house, each giving points from their own device. Somebody invited as a visitor can give points and nothing else: they cannot take any away, approve spending, or change how the family works.

Your children are never the paywall. All of them, free, either way.

What comes with Sherbet Family?

Two things. Up to ten grown-ups, each giving points from their own phone in their own name — a second parent, grandparents, whoever helps. And five of the seven games; two are there without paying. It is one payment, once, and everybody you invite is covered by it.

Everything else is in the free version and stays there: every child with no limit, all of the points, the reasons, the rewards, what they have asked for, the children's own side, the character builder, the widget, iCloud between your own devices, and getting your data out.

My child changed their character and I preferred the old one

It is theirs. Ask them nicely.

Can I get my data out?

It is already yours — it lives in your iCloud, not ours. There is also a plain readable file: the gear, then Settings → Export the whole family. Bring a family back, on the same screen, reads one in again.

Something is broken

Tell us what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. That is genuinely enough, and it is more useful than a screenshot on its own.
